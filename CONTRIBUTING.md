# Contributing to Orden

Pull requests target the `unstable` branch.

## Contributor licence agreement

Before a contribution can be merged, its author signs a contributor licence agreement (CLA). You keep the copyright in your work. You grant the project a licence to use it and to license it under other terms, and the project commits to also keep it available under the licence Orden used on the day you contributed (currently AGPL-3.0).

The agreements are the Harmony Contributor License Agreements 1.0, with only the blanks filled in:

- [Individual agreement](legal/cla/HA-CLA-I.md), if you contribute in your own name.
- [Entity agreement](legal/cla/HA-CLA-E.md), if you contribute for a company or other legal entity.

[legal/cla/README.md](legal/cla/README.md) lists how the template was filled in.

### How to sign

Individuals: open your pull request. A bot comments on it with the agreement and how to sign with your GitHub account. You sign once, and it covers your later contributions too. The pull request cannot be merged until every author of its commits has signed.

Companies: say so in the pull request. A person authorised to sign for the company signs the entity agreement, and the maintainer then records which GitHub accounts it covers.

If you are employed and the work may belong to your employer, have your employer approve the individual agreement or sign the entity agreement.

### Work you do not own

Contribute only work you wrote yourself and hold the copyright in. If a pull request contains anything written by someone else, such as copied code, assets or text:

1. Say so in the pull request description, with the source, the author and the licence.
2. Keep the original copyright and licence notices.
3. Include it only if its licence allows use in an AGPL-3.0 project.

The CLA does not cover such parts. The maintainer decides whether they can be included.
